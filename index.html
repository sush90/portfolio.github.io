Research that measures models and the people they affect

I'm a Computer Science and Data Science student at Bucknell University, and I work where machine learning meets decisions that affect real people. My research pairs quantitative modeling with qualitative analysis, because accuracy alone never tells you whether a model is fair or whether it understood what people meant.

For one project I rebuilt the risk-assessment models used by the Pennsylvania Department of Corrections and audited them for predictive accuracy and bias across race, gender, and age. For another, I lead a team that uses large language models and transformer-based NLP (BERT, RoBERTa) to code open-ended student survey responses, then measures how closely the AI's labels match human coders.

I'm now earning a Google Cloud certification, building on coursework in AI and cloud computing, so this work can move from notebooks to scalable, cloud-deployed pipelines. I'm looking for 2027 new-grad roles in data science, AI/ML, and software engineering.
